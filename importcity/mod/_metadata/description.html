Choose a real place. Turn it into a Transport Fever 3 map. Redefine the transport strategy.

RealMap builds a map of a real-world area in the Map Editor: relief, sea, rivers and lakes, biomes, towns at their real size, named industries, road links and a railway, all from open data.

⚠ This mod works with the free RealMap app

The map is prepared by the RealMap desktop app for Windows, free and open source. The mod does the in-game part: it builds everything in the Map Editor, step by step, while the app shows the progress.

➜ Download the RealMap app

Features

  • Any place in the world: pick the game map size and format, the scale (1:1 to 1:8) and the centre on a map.
  • Real relief and water: precise elevation data (IGN in France, Copernicus elsewhere), sea floor, rivers that stay continuous and navigable, gentle banks, lakes and marshes.
  • Biomes from the real land cover: forests, fields, scrub, rock, beaches without trees.
  • Towns at their real size, with commercial and industrial districts and starting goods drawn far from the factories that make them.
  • Industries from real industrial sites and land cover, named after the real ones, following the game's density and economy.
  • Road links between towns (the game lays the roads) and a railway designed for the game's relief: curves, gradients, bridges and tunnels.
  • Your choices: add or remove towns, industries, road links and railway lines with a right click.

Installation

  1. Subscribe to this mod.
  2. Install the RealMap app from the download page (Windows, Linux or macOS; not signed yet: on Windows, SmartScreen › "More info" › "Run anyway").
  3. Start the app: it finds the game's data folder by itself, or choose it in the Export tab (with Steam: Steam folder › userdata › your Steam ID › 3493540 › local).

How to use it

  1. In the app, tab 1: choose the map size, format, scale and area, then Get the data.
  2. Check the result on the map; adjust it in tab 2 (advanced options) or with a right click if you like.
  3. In the game: Map Editor › new empty map of the size and format shown by the app, with RealMap enabled in the mods.
  4. In the app, tab 3: Generate the map. The mod builds relief, biomes, railway, towns, industries and roads by itself; the app shows each step.
  5. Save the map from the Map Editor menu.

Good to know

  • Map Editor only: the mod does nothing in a running game.
  • No server, no account: the app and the mod talk through files in the game's data folder. The app needs Internet to fetch the open data.
  • The roads follow the game's own routing between the planned towns; the railway is the main real line (plus the lines you add), without stations or trains.
  • The town count is capped by the game's limit for the map size; very big or "experimental" map sizes are slower to build.
  • The first data download can take a few minutes (OpenStreetMap servers); later changes reuse the cache.
  • German, Italian, Spanish and Chinese are machine translations with context: corrections are welcome.
