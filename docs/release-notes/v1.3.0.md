title: RealMap 1.3.0 — whole countries: towns, water and industries from Geofabrik extracts

**Update the app and the mod** (Mod Hub or `realmap-mod-1.3.0.zip`, then restart the game). The mod only changes its version number, which must match the app's.

## Whole countries
- **Very large areas** (over 20,000 km², such as a whole country) no longer use the OpenStreetMap servers (Overpass), which are not made for this and sent back empty answers without saying so: no towns, no water. RealMap now downloads the [Geofabrik](https://download.geofabrik.de) extracts that cover the area (regions or countries) and reads them on your computer.
  - Before anything is downloaded, RealMap lists the regions with the size of each and the share of the area it covers, and the total to download (several GB for a whole country). Untick the ones you do not need; heavy neighbouring countries that only touch a thin strip of the map start unticked, but you can tick them back.
  - Each region shows its estimated time (download and reading), learnt from your own connection and computer, and the list shows the total.
  - Each region is read on your computer as soon as it is downloaded, while the next one downloads. The download shows its progress, resumes after a cut, and the files stay in the cache for 30 days: the next maps in the same area start at once, and ticking another region only reads that one.
  - Towns, water, industrial sites, roads and railways are the same as with Overpass (checked region by region).
- **Memory**: the relief of a very large area is read at a resolution that suits the map, and the OpenStreetMap shapes are simplified to the map's precision. A whole country needs a few hundred megabytes instead of several gigabytes. Usual maps are not affected.
- **Sea floor**: the EMODnet sea floor of a very large area is requested at a suitable resolution instead of failing and silently falling back to coarser data.

## Lists of towns and industries
- **You choose when the map is updated**: ticking towns, industries, roads or railways no longer recomputes the map each time. The Lists panel counts the changes waiting and has an « Update the map » button; tick « Update automatically » for the old behaviour.
- **Many at once**: filter the towns by name, type (city, town, village) and minimum population, or the industries by name, type and origin, then « Tick the N » or « Untick the N » in one click. A whole country has tens of thousands of places: the list shows the first 300, the buttons act on the whole filter.
- The Lists panel is wider and can be resized with its right edge.

## Usual areas (Overpass)
- **Towns no longer depend on the water**: places have their own light request. If the water request fails, you get an error instead of a map without towns.
- RealMap follows the Overpass rules more closely: it asks for the resources it needs (so busy servers accept the request sooner), pauses 30 s when asked to slow down, never runs more requests at once than allowed, and an answer cut short by the server is detected, never kept in the cache, and requested again in smaller pieces.

## Other fixes
- Download caches are written safely: an interrupted download no longer leaves a broken file that blocked the next imports. Empty answers kept by earlier versions are downloaded again.
- If the data of an area is too large for the project file, the project is saved without it (a warning says so); the data is read again from the cache when the project is opened.
- The technical journal says when a download falls back to another source or is cut short.

Thanks to the players who reported the Poland imports!
