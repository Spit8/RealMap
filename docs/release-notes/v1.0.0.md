title: RealMap 1.0.0 — first public release

**RealMap** turns a real place into a Transport Fever 3 map: relief, sea, rivers and lakes, biomes, towns at their real size, named industries, road links and a railway, built for you in the game's Map Editor.

## Highlights
- **Any place in the world**: game map size and format, scale from 1:1 to 1:8, area picked on a map.
- **Open data**: relief from IGN RGE ALTI (France) or Copernicus GLO-30, sea floor from EMODnet or ETOPO, land cover from ESA WorldCover, water, places, industrial sites, roads and railways from OpenStreetMap. Everything is cached.
- **Water made for the game**: continuous, navigable rivers with gentle banks, deep enough rivers, marshes and coasts, beaches without trees.
- **Towns** at their real size, with commercial and industrial shares from the real business areas, and starting goods drawn far from the factories that make them.
- **Industries** from real sites and land cover, named after the real ones, following the game's density and economy.
- **Road links** between towns, chosen automatically (each town linked to 1 or 2 others) or by hand with a right click; the game lays the roads.
- **Railway** following the real main line, adjusted to the game's relief (along the banks rather than across the rivers), with curve, gradient, bridge and tunnel design.
- **Automatic generation**: open a new empty map in the Map Editor, click *Generate the map*, and the RealMap mod builds everything step by step while the app shows the progress.
- **Six languages** in the app and the mod.

## Installation
- **App**: Windows `RealMap_1.0.0_x64-setup.exe` (or the `.msi`, or `RealMap_1.0.0_x64-portable.zip` without installation), Linux `.AppImage` or `.deb`, macOS `.dmg`. Tested on Windows; the Linux and macOS builds have not been tried yet. The installers are not signed yet: SmartScreen › *More info* › *Run anyway*, Gatekeeper › right click › *Open*.
- **Mod**: subscribe to **RealMap** in the game's Mod Hub (mod.io), or unzip `realmap-mod-1.0.0.zip` into the game's `mods` folder (Steam: `<Steam>\userdata\<SteamID>\3493540\local\mods\`).

See the [README](https://github.com/Spit8/RealMap#readme) for the full guide and the known limitations.
