title: RealMap 1.1.0 — turn the area, large maps, reliable relief downloads

**Update the app.** The mod only changes its version number (Mod Hub or `realmap-mod-1.1.0.zip`, then restart the game).

## New
- **Turn the area**: hold the orange handle in the middle of the area and drag; the top of the game map points to the mouse (Shift: 15° steps). The angle can also be typed, to the tenth of a degree, in *Area and data › Rotation*, and « North up » puts it back. Relief, water, towns, industries, roads and the preview all follow the turned area.

## Fixes
- **Large maps** (about 300 × 300 km and more): the preview stopped with « Failed to execute 'postMessage' on 'Worker': Data cannot be cloned, out of memory ». The data now goes to the computation in pieces, and the elevation tiles are cut to the area first. Thanks for the report!
- **Relief downloads**: a dropped connection (« Failed to fetch ») no longer stops the data step; each tile is tried again (the step shows the countdown). A tile that arrived incomplete and then failed every time (« incorrect data check ») is detected, deleted and downloaded again. When IGN does not answer, Copernicus fills the relief instead of stopping.

## Installation
Same as 1.0.0: see the [README](https://github.com/Spit8/RealMap#readme). Installed apps update themselves from the Export tab.
