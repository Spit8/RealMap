title: RealMap 1.2.1 — the experimental map sizes, a railway that keeps off the roads, the map follows the opened project

**Update the app and the mod** (Mod Hub or `realmap-mod-1.2.1.zip`, then restart the game).

## New
- **Map sizes beyond Very large**: Huge, Megalomaniac, Gigantomaniac (up to 28.7 × 28.7 km), Tiny and the 1 : 4 and 1 : 5 formats are always listed. The game offers them only with `experimentalMapFeatures = true` in its `settings.lua` (game closed, PC, 16 GB of RAM advised): while that is off, they are greyed out and a link opens the file. RealMap reads the file again when its window gets the focus back, no need to restart the app. A project saved with an experimental size keeps it; the export waits until the game offers that size. Thanks for the report!
- **A railway that keeps off the roads**: the line no longer weaves from one side of a road to the other. It foresees the roads the game will build between the linked towns and stays on one side, crossing them only where it has to. It may now leave the real line by up to 1,000 m (new projects; *Advanced options › Distance from the real line*, up to 2,000 m).

## Fixes
- **Railway under the roads** (mod): the generation could stop at the railway (« attempt to perform arithmetic on field '?' ») when a line met several roads. The track under a road no longer makes a kink (« Excessive curvature »), the tunnel grows when a road comes too close to its mouth or a few roads are close together, the track climbs back between them instead of staying deep, and a piece refused in the cutting is tried again as a tunnel, not as a bridge.
- **Opening a project** (or switching to another one, or the projects of the last session at start) now centres the map on its area. Thanks for the report!

## Installation
Same as 1.0.0: see the [README](https://github.com/Spit8/RealMap#readme). Installed apps update themselves from the Export tab.
