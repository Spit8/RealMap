title: RealMap 1.2.0 — map projects, rivers and lakes on demand, separate road networks, the railway under the roads

**Update the app and the mod** (Mod Hub or `realmap-mod-1.2.0.zip`, then restart the game).

## New
- **Map projects**: each map is now a project you can keep. **File › Save** (Ctrl+S) writes a `.realmap` file in the folder you choose, with the settings, the downloaded data cut to the area, the result and the log: it reopens at once, even offline. **File** also has New, Open, Save as and Close (Ctrl+N, Ctrl+O, Ctrl+Shift+S, Ctrl+W).
- **Projet tab**: the open projects as a tree (✱ = changes not saved; click to switch, right-click to save or close), with the log below it. The app reopens the projects of the last session and asks before closing a project, or the app, with changes not saved.
- **Rivers and lakes can be turned off** (*Advanced options › Water*): the game has a single water level, so rivers and lakes are dug down to it and their valleys lowered. Unticked, only the sea stays and river beds and lake floors keep their real relief. Thanks for the idea!
- **Separate road networks at the start**: towns are linked in small networks (3 towns at most by default, *Advanced options › Roads*) for you to join in game, never a lone town: each town has another town or an industry. Each industry is shown linked to its nearest town.

## Changes in the game (mod)
- **The railway goes under the roads**: the mod now builds the roads before the railway (the game's road builder gives up on any track in its way), then lowers the track into a short tunnel under each road it crosses, with approaches at the line's gradient.
- The report lists the industries the game left without a road nearby (industries on water are never linked by the game).

## Fixes
- **No road between towns** after moving the area: road links changed by hand on a previous map froze the choice and left the new map without any link. Changing the area now starts again from the automatic choice.

## Installation
Same as 1.0.0: see the [README](https://github.com/Spit8/RealMap#readme). Installed apps update themselves from the Export tab.
